I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the **University of Maryland, College Park** (GPA: 3.9/4.0), advised by [Professor Ang Li](https://www.ang-li.com/) in the [CASE Lab](https://www.ang-li.com/lab/). I expect to complete my M.S. in 2026.

My research focuses on **efficient and robust deep learning** for vision–language models (VLMs) and vision–language–action models (VLAs) on edge and robotic platforms. I work at the intersection of model compression and deployment-constrained perception — studying how quantization, out-of-distribution robustness, and accuracy–memory–latency trade-offs interact in real systems.

**Current projects:**
- Designing systematic post-training quantization (PTQ) pipelines for VLM architectures, with a focus on calibration-data sensitivity across modalities
- Developing a feature-space rectification method for OOD-robust quantized perception on SWaP-constrained edge platforms using frozen-backbone LoRA adaptation

Prior to UMD, I was a Research Assistant at the **University of Tehran** (2022–2023), where I worked on cache-replacement policies for edge networks using federated reinforcement learning and multi-armed bandit frameworks.

I hold a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from **Sharif University of Technology** (GPA: 3.61/4.0).
